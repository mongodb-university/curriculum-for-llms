6. So where does MongoDB fit into this landscape? When we integrate MongoDB with AI agents, we create systems that can think, remember, and learn from experiences.

Or perhaps you want an agent that can analyze trends across thousands of documents in your collection to deliver meaningful insights. These are simple examples, but they give you a basic idea of what's possible.
